## How this coffee differs from El Perezoso

Worth noting before dialing, because the differences point in a useful direction:

| | El Perezoso | Timaná |
|---|---|---|
| Roast | Medium | **Light/Medium** |
| Altitude | 1,300–1,600 m | **1,600–1,650 m** |
| Process | Natural | Natural, **100 h anaerobic** |
| Score | 85 | 86 |
| Espresso spec | 1:2.3, 27 s | **1:2, 25–30 s** |

Higher-grown and lighter-roasted — denser, harder to extract. Expect to grind **finer** than El Perezoso, not coarser. Anaerobic fermentation typically adds a fruit-forward intensity that reads as acidity if under-extracted.

**Turbo shot candidacy:** better than El Perezoso. Light/medium + high-grown + dense is closer to turbo's documented domain. Worth a proper attempt once a conventional baseline exists.

## Starting points

**Filter (recommended first):**
- 22 g / 330 g (1:15) — deeper bed than the roaster's 20 g, more forgiving on the Switch
- Grind 1/30 on the Arco, adjust from there
- Boiling water (roaster says 93 °C — try both)
- 50 g bloom, 45 s, Switch open
- Target drawdown 3:00–3:30
- **Sveti Rok spring water** — not tap

**Espresso:**
- 15.0 g in the VST 15 g basket
- Roaster's 1:2 = 30 g out
- Start conventional: PP95/Pr7, grind fine enough for 9 bar
- Establish a baseline that tastes right *before* attempting turbo
